Root-Defense — Tamper-Evident Security Logs

3rd Prize Winner · ₹10,000 Award · Team Flute
Tech Habba 2026 // Acharya Institute of Technology

Root-Defense

Quai Network Anchor

Tamper-evident security logs that an attacker with root access can't quietly erase.

Each log line is hashed, signed and sent to an off-host witness as it's written. Batches fold into a Merkle root anchored on an EVM smart contract on Quai Network. An auditor portal recomputes everything and shows Genuine or Tampered with the exact entry that changed. A 5-second heartbeat triggers an alert if the agent goes silent, while a forward-secure key ratchet makes stolen keys useless for re-signing past history.

01. The Problem

Once attackers gain root access on a server, they can quietly rewrite or wipe auth, syslog, and sudo records that expose their intrusion. Following a breach, proving which logs are genuine to auditors or in court becomes impossible.

180 Days Log Retention (CERT-In) 6 Hours Incident Window

02. The Approach

We can't stop root from editing local files. We make every edit detectable, locatable, and provable. Logs leave the server in seconds, fold into Merkle roots anchored to Quai Network, and verify via a one-click auditor portal.

Proof-of-Work EVM Hardhat / Anvil Fallback

Privacy Guarantee: Only cryptographic hashes, Merkle roots, and heartbeat digests reach the public blockchain. Raw log contents remain encrypted on the off-host witness, accessible solely by authorized auditors.
